# The tray icon

The Stowaway icon in the notification area (bottom right, next to the clock) shows the protection status at a glance (for example *100% protected* or *2 backups failing*) and pops up a notice when backups start failing. Right-click it to open Stowaway, **Activity** or **Snapshots**. Double-click opens the dashboard.

Windows puts new icons in the overflow area: click the **^** next to the clock to find it, and drag it onto the taskbar to keep it visible. It starts when the installer finishes and whenever you sign in; if it's missing, sign out of Windows and back in.
